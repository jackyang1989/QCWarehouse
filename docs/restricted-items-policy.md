# QC Warehouse Restricted Items Policy

**Effective date** 24 November 2026

## 1. Check before you ship

QC Warehouse receives physical goods in mainland China for the inspection, comparison, and handling services shown in your order. Use your current receiving address and personal receiving code. Before asking a seller to ship, check that your item and its contents are eligible for the selected service and accepted by the carrier for that journey.

Apparel is our first available inspection category. Availability for a particular item depends on the current service catalog and the agreed checks. Ask us before shipping an item if its category, material, components, or handling needs are unclear. Include the item name, quantity, condition, materials, and any relevant product label or specification. A receiving code or tracking number identifies your parcel and does not establish eligibility.

## 2. Items we do not accept

Do not send goods that are illegal to possess, circulate, or ship, or goods that infringe intellectual property rights, including counterfeit products. We may stop handling an item while we verify a reasonable concern about its legality or ownership.

We do not accept weapons, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, flammable or corrosive materials, toxic or radioactive substances, illegal drugs, infectious material, or other hazardous or legally prohibited goods. We do not accept cash, negotiable instruments, live animals, perishables, or human or animal specimens. This list gives examples. Applicable law and the carrier's rules also govern what may be shipped.

## 3. Items requiring advance review

Do not send batteries, power banks, items containing batteries, liquids, gels, powders, aerosols, cosmetics, medicines, food, or goods that require special packaging or storage unless QC Warehouse has confirmed in writing that the specific item can be received and handled for your order. A product packed together with a restricted accessory is subject to the same review. A carrier or delivery route may impose additional restrictions, even where an item can be safely received.

Send accurate descriptions to us and the carrier. Do not conceal restricted components or label the contents as something else. Give us the seller's original tracking number and any information we need to identify the contents safely.

## 4. If a parcel is refused or held

If we cannot safely or lawfully receive, inspect, store, or dispatch an item, we may refuse receipt or pause work on the affected items. If we already hold the parcel, we may secure or separate it and request accurate descriptions, proof of entitlement, or instructions. We will use the contact details on your account to explain the issue and available options when we can legally do so.

For items that may lawfully be released, we prioritize safe return or another authorized handover. A carrier or authority may direct different handling. A hold or an expired storage period does not by itself transfer ownership to us or authorize sale or destruction. Any disposal requires a documented basis and appropriate approval. When applicable law requires reporting or handover, we will follow the requirements of the competent authority.

## 5. Returns, costs, and help

If you want a seller return, first arrange it with the seller and give us the seller's designated domestic return address and any required contact details. If the goods can lawfully be returned and a carrier accepts them, we can arrange domestic dispatch after you approve the applicable charges. You pay the return postage. We record the carrier and tracking number after dispatch. The seller handles your purchase refund or replacement separately.

Our service refunds follow the Terms of Service. If you are unsure whether an item can be sent or you have already arranged shipment, contact support@qcwarehouse.com with your order or tracking reference.
