# QC Warehouse Privacy Policy

**Effective date** 24 November 2026

## 1. Who handles your information

QC Warehouse is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. This policy applies when you visit qcwarehouse.com, sign in, contact us, or use our product receiving, inspection, reporting, storage, or domestic dispatch services.

For privacy inquiries and requests, contact our **Privacy Contact** at support@qcwarehouse.com. Put “Privacy request” in your email subject so we can route it to the right person.

## 2. Information we collect and why

| Information | When and why we use it |
| --- | --- |
| Account and contact information | Your email, display name or preferences you provide, receiving code, account identifiers, consent records, and sign-in and verification history. We use these to create and secure your account, identify your parcels, and communicate with you about services. |
| Google or Microsoft sign-in | When you choose a provider, we request standard OpenID Connect identity information under the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes. We use your provider's account identifier and verified contact email to create, recognize, or link your QC Warehouse account. We may ask you to verify an email separately if the provider has not established it for our purposes. |
| Orders and physical goods | Product descriptions, supplier or product references you provide, item identifiers, the checks you commission, photographs, measurements, and report files. We use these to match items to your orders, carry out checks, deliver reports, and resolve issues. |
| Parcels, recipients, and tracking | Receiving codes, carrier and tracking numbers, shipping labels, and the names, addresses, and contact numbers needed to match incoming parcels or deliver authorized shipments. A carrier or tracking service may require limited phone verification. We use these details to identify parcels, process your shipping instructions, and show available tracking updates. |
| Payments, refunds, and disputes | Order references, amounts, currency, transaction references, payment and refund status, and limited payment details provided by the payment processor. When you use hosted Stripe checkout, you enter card details with Stripe. We use transaction information to confirm payments, keep financial records, issue refunds, and investigate disputes. |
| Inquiries, account recovery, and support | Your messages, contact details, identifiers, and documents you choose to submit or that we need to verify a claim or recovery request. We use these to respond, verify authorization, and resolve the issue. Keep passwords, complete card numbers, and unrelated identity documents out of ordinary support messages. |
| Website and security activity | IP address, browser or device information, time and path of access, security events, and records of significant account or operations actions. We use these to keep the site operating, investigate incidents, prevent abuse, and audit changes to accounts, payments, reports, and items. |

We receive information from you, the sign-in or payment provider you choose, carriers and their tracking systems, the labels on packages sent to our facility, and the records that our staff create while providing the service. Staff may use text recognition to help read a label and verify its tracking or receiving information. We mark the information needed for an action when we ask for it. For example, we need a usable contact email to create or recover an account, sufficient shipment details to verify a parcel, and the necessary recipient details to dispatch an item. Without those details, we may be unable to perform the requested action.

If you give us another person's details, such as a seller's return contact or a recipient's address, provide only what we need for that shipment. Make sure you are entitled to share the details and inform that person where required.

## 3. Use of Google and Microsoft data

We use sign-in data to verify your identity, associate the provider with your QC Warehouse account, and protect account access. We store the provider's account identifier and account linkage information, and use the applicable email as an account or contact address. Sign-in permissions cover basic identity. Access to Google or Microsoft mailboxes, messages, contacts, calendars, and stored files is outside those permissions.

We do not use or sell Google or Microsoft sign-in data for advertising. Access to sign-in data is limited to the personnel and services that need it for account operation, security, support, or a legal obligation. You can request removal of a linked sign-in method through your account or by contacting us. If it is your only working method, you may need to verify another method first to keep access. Disconnecting an app at your provider and closing your QC Warehouse account are separate actions.

## 4. Who receives information

We give providers the information they need for the relevant function. Depending on the service you use, data may be disclosed to the following recipients.

- **Sign-in providers.** Google or Microsoft processes your request to authenticate when you choose its sign-in button. Its own privacy terms also govern how it handles your account.
- **Payment processors.** Stripe processes checkout, payment, refunds, and payment disputes when Stripe is available for your transaction. The processor handles information it collects from you under its own privacy terms.
- **Communications providers.** Our transactional email provider, Postmark, processes recipient addresses, message content needed for delivery, and delivery or bounce events when the email service is enabled.
- **Warehouse and logistics recipients.** Authorized receiving and inspection personnel, domestic carriers, and tracking services such as Kuaidi100 receive the details they need for their part of the job. A seller or forwarder you designate may receive the recipient or shipment information accompanying your authorized dispatch.
- **Infrastructure and security providers.** Web hosting, database and backup infrastructure, and Cloudflare, which helps deliver and protect the website, process data to operate the service. Staff access is limited according to their responsibilities.
- **Advisers and authorities.** We may disclose information where reasonably necessary for legal, financial, or professional advice, to deal with a claim, or to meet a lawful request or obligation.

Inspection evidence and reports are restricted to authorized accounts and staff. If report sharing is available and you choose to generate a link, recipients with access to that link can view the specified report version within its access period. Share it only with people you intend to give access to. You can revoke the link where the sharing feature provides that control.

## 5. Processing in different locations

Our physical receiving, inspection, and dispatch work takes place in mainland China. Depending on the service involved, data may also be processed in other places where our hosting, payment, identity, communications, or tracking providers operate. We apply access restrictions and use transfer arrangements required by applicable law. A provider handling your identity or payment directly may give you its own information about where it processes data.

## 6. Cookies and device storage

We use cookies and browser storage for sign-in sessions, account registration, security checks, preferences, and functions such as restoring an unfinished draft. Infrastructure and security services may also set cookies necessary to deliver or protect the site. You can manage cookies through your browser. Blocking required cookies may interrupt authentication, registration, or account functions.

## 7. Retention and security

We keep account information while it is needed to provide your account and support services. We keep order, shipment, report, and evidence records for the time needed to deliver services, investigate issues, and handle claims. We retain legally required financial and tax records for the applicable period. We limit retention of security, verification, and support information according to its purpose and applicable obligations.

When records are no longer needed, we take steps to delete or de-identify them. Information required for outstanding shipments, claims, refunds, security investigations, or legal obligations may be retained after account closure. Copies in backups are retained and removed under the applicable backup schedule. We use measures such as encrypted connections, access restrictions, and security logging to protect personal data.

## 8. Your choices and requests

You may request access to or correction of your personal data by writing to the Privacy Contact above. You may also ask to close your account, remove a linked sign-in method, obtain a copy of certain records, or request deletion or other restrictions that applicable law permits. We may ask you to confirm your identity and, where you act for someone else, your authority. We will explain any information that we must keep and why.

If the law in your location provides additional privacy rights, you may exercise them through the same contact. You may raise a concern with the relevant privacy regulator.

## 9. Policy changes

We publish updates at https://qcwarehouse.com/legal/privacy with a new effective date. For material changes, we will provide notice or seek any further consent required by applicable law. Questions about this policy can be sent to support@qcwarehouse.com.
