# QC Warehouse Inspection Report Limitations

**Effective date** 24 November 2026

## 1. What the report covers

Your report covers the physical units identified in the report and the checks agreed for your order. Depending on the service and product category, it may include visual observations, measurements, photos, comparisons, and findings against agreed criteria. The report identifies the items examined, the method or checkpoint where applicable, and any agreed check we could not complete.

Standard inspection records the checks performed on each commissioned unit. Sample comparison applies the same agreed checkpoints to the designated samples. Reference sample matching compares the submitted items with the reference identified in the order. An inspection and report concern the specific units received. Results cannot establish the condition or quality of other units, future production, or a supplier's entire batch.

## 2. How to read findings and measurements

Read each finding together with the item ID, inspection date, checkpoint, method, units, and any stated tolerance. An observation that no issue was recorded means no issue was recorded under that check at the time. A skipped, inaccessible, or inconclusive check provides no result for that characteristic. Photos illustrate the selected areas and conditions seen during inspection.

Color, texture, and finish may appear different under different lighting or camera and display settings. Measurements can vary with product positioning, tension, equipment, and the method used. A comparison describes observed similarities or differences under the stated method. A match against an agreed reference covers only the listed criteria and tolerance. Damage or changes after inspection are outside the scope of the findings.

## 3. What needs separate evidence

QC Warehouse provides warehouse-based product inspection and comparison within the agreed scope. The report is not a laboratory test, product certification, factory audit, or regulatory compliance determination. Visual observations and basic measurements cannot establish product safety, chemical composition, authenticity, hidden defects, long-term durability, or suitability for a particular use.

Use an appropriately qualified provider for mandatory tests, certifications, factory assessments, or other checks beyond the service you purchased. Do not present an inspection result, photograph, report extract, or receipt as proof that a product or supplier has been certified or legally approved.

## 4. Using or sharing the report

Use the report to help compare samples, discuss observed findings with your seller, or decide how to handle the inspected goods. You may share relevant findings with your seller or authorized team. Keep the report version, sample identity, relevant method, and limitations with any extract. Avoid edits, cropping, or captions that change the meaning of a finding or imply that an uninspected product was covered.

Treat report files and images as private. Before sharing beyond the people involved in your purchase or inspection, check your authority to disclose supplier and third-party material, and remove any personal information that the recipient does not need. Share links only with their intended recipients. Use any expiration or revocation controls available for that link.

## 5. Corrections and follow-up

If an item seems misidentified or a finding appears incomplete or incorrect, contact support@qcwarehouse.com with the order number, report version, item ID, and the check or photo in question. We may review the recorded work and evidence and issue a revised version if a correction is warranted. Refer to the identified version in any subsequent discussion with a seller or other authorized recipient.

The report does not decide whether a seller must accept a return or give a refund. Those requests are made to your seller. To ask us to handle, store, or dispatch items after inspection, use the applicable options in your QC Warehouse account.
